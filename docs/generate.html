Create your application

Follow these steps to turn your spreadsheet into an application.

Step 1 — Prepare your spreadsheet

Before importing your data:

  1. Make sure the first column is named ID.
  2. If IDs are missing, enter 1 and 2 in the first two data rows.
  3. Select both cells and double-click the fill handle to extend the numeric series.
  4. Check that every record has a unique positive integer ID. Keep existing valid IDs unchanged.

Step 2 — Connect your Google Sheet

Copy the URL from your Google Sheets browser address bar and paste it below. The spreadsheet must be accessible for import. Do not make confidential data public.
